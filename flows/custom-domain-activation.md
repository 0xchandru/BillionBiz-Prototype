# Flow: Store and Custom Domain Activation

1. Authorized merchant selects Online Store → Domains.
2. Merchant requests a platform subdomain or enters a custom domain they control.
3. System normalizes host, checks reserved/duplicate/conflicting records and creates `DNS_PENDING` mapping.
4. Show DNS records generated by actual deployment provider; do not invent values.
5. Merchant adds TXT/CNAME/A records at DNS provider.
6. Verification worker checks ownership and routing records.
7. Certificate provisioning begins; display certificate status separately from ownership verification.
8. Only after hostname and TLS states pass does domain become `ACTIVE` and serve the correct store.
9. Apply canonical-domain redirects, previous-domain redirects and SEO updates safely.
10. Monitor renewal/validation; alert on expiry/loss; detach safely before host reassignment.
